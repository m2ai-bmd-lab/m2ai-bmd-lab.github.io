Måle. Forstå. Forbedre liv.

We capture human behaviour, physiology and cognition at the same time: optical motion capture as ground truth, synchronized with muscle activity, brain activity, eye tracking, thermal imaging, radio-frequency sensing, XR and wearables, and processed on site with edge AI. We are also adding imaging and brain-health measures: low-field MRI, point-of-care ultrasound and transcranial Doppler (TCD).

Det som skiller laben

Alle modaliteter, tatt opp samtidig, mot én fasit.

Most labs measure one signal at a time. Here, motion, muscle, brain, gaze, heat and radio can all be recorded in the same session and aligned on a single timeline, with optical motion capture as the reference that every other measure is validated against. That is what lets us build and test multimodal AI on real people, not on stitched-together datasets.

Fra signaler til multimodal KI

  1. 01

    Måle

    Motion capture, EMG, EEG, eye tracking, thermal, RF and wearables on the same participant.

  2. 02

    Synkronisere

    Hardware TTL triggers and Lab Streaming Layer put every stream on one clock.

  3. 03

    Validere

    Sub-millimetre motion-capture data is the ground truth for every other measure.

  4. 04

    Lære

    Edge AI computers for real-time inference; the GPU workstation for training and analysis.
